# Privacy Policy — Overcomers Global Network

**Effective date:** 2026-06-16
**Last updated:** 2026-06-16

This Privacy Policy describes how Overcomers Global Network ("OGN", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Overcomers Global Network mobile application (the "App") and our website at overcomersglobalnetwork.com (the "Site"). By using the App or the Site, you agree to this Policy.

## 1. Who we are

Overcomers Global Network is a ministry that publishes the App for worship, Bible study, prayer, community, and outreach. You can reach us at:

- Email: support@overcomersglobalnetwork.com
- Web: https://overcomersglobalnetwork.com

## 2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the App and serve our community.

**You give us:**
- Name and email address when you create an account.
- Profile details you choose to add (display name, profile photo).
- Prayer requests, chat messages, testimonies, and other content you post.
- Outreach notes and map pins you log in the Evangelism Maps feature.
- Messages you send our support team.

**We collect automatically:**
- Device type, OS version, and app version (for crash diagnostics).
- Anonymous crash and performance data.
- Approximate or precise location — **only when you actively choose to log a visit** on the Evangelism Maps feature. We do not track your location in the background.

**We do not collect:**
- Payment card numbers. Giving is processed by our giving partner (Stripe) on the website at overcomersglobalnetwork.com/give. Card data never touches the App.
- Advertising identifiers. The App does not show ads and does not participate in cross-app or cross-site tracking.

## 3. How we use your information

- To provide the App's features: accounts, Bible reading, prayer feed, community chat, evangelism mapping, events.
- To moderate community spaces and enforce our community guidelines.
- To notify you of live services, answered prayers, and important updates (you can turn notifications off in your device settings or Profile).
- To diagnose crashes and improve the App.
- To respond to your support requests.

## 4. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

- **Service providers** who help us operate the App:
  - **Supabase** — authentication and data storage.
  - **Expo / Expo Application Services** — app updates and crash reporting.
  - **Stripe** — giving (only when you tap a giving link and complete the transaction on Stripe's hosted page).
  - **Apple and Google** — app distribution and required diagnostics.
- **Other users** — content you post in public areas of the App (prayer feed, community chat, group channels) is visible to other signed-in members of those areas.
- **Legal and safety** — when required by law, or when needed to protect rights, safety, or our community.
- **Ministry transfers** — if OGN transfers operations of the App to a successor ministry, your information may transfer with it; this Policy continues to apply.

## 5. Your choices

- **Edit your profile** — Profile tab in the App.
- **Turn off notifications** — Profile tab, or in your device's system settings.
- **Stop location collection** — Decline the location permission, or revoke it in your device's system settings. The map will still load; only logging a visit requires location.
- **Delete your account and data** — From the Profile tab, choose "Request Account Deletion", or email support@overcomersglobalnetwork.com. We will delete your account, prayer history, chat messages, profile, and outreach logs within 30 days, except where law requires us to retain limited records.
- **Request a copy of your data** — Email support@overcomersglobalnetwork.com and we will send you an export within 30 days.

## 6. Data retention

- Account, profile, and content: kept while your account is active.
- Crash and diagnostic logs: kept up to 90 days.
- Deleted accounts and content: removed within 30 days of your request, except where law requires longer retention.

## 7. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards: HTTPS / TLS in transit, encryption at rest in our managed database, and access controls on our administrative tools. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password and tell us at security@overcomersglobalnetwork.com if you believe your account has been compromised.

## 8. Children

The App is intended for general audiences ages 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, contact support@overcomersglobalnetwork.com and we will delete it.

## 9. International users

OGN operates from the United States. If you use the App from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate.

## 10. Your rights (region-specific)

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict our use of your personal information, and the right to data portability. To exercise these rights, contact support@overcomersglobalnetwork.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

- **California (CCPA/CPRA):** We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
- **EU / UK (GDPR / UK GDPR):** Our legal bases are: your consent (for optional features such as location and notifications), the performance of our user agreement, and our legitimate ministry interests in operating and securing the App. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 11. Changes to this Policy

We will post any changes to this Policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be highlighted in the App.

## 12. Contact

Questions? Requests? Email **support@overcomersglobalnetwork.com**.
