## Common questions

### How do I create an account?
Open the app, tap **Get Started**, and enter your name, email, and a password. Check your inbox for a confirmation link.

### I forgot my password.
Tap **Sign In → Forgot password?** and enter the email you used to sign up. We'll send you a reset link.

### How do I delete my account?
Open the **Profile** tab and tap **Request Account Deletion**. Or email support@overcomersglobalnetwork.com and we'll handle it. Your account and personal data are removed within 30 days.

### The live stream isn't loading.
Live streams are only active during scheduled services. Outside those windows, browse recorded services under the **Messages** tab. If a live stream is scheduled and still won't play, restart the app and check your internet connection.

### The Bible only shows one verse.
Tap the **Chapter / Verse** switch at the top of the Bible tab and choose **Chapter** to read the whole chapter.

### My prayer request didn't post.
Pull down to refresh the Prayer feed. If it still doesn't appear, sign out and back in. If the problem continues, email support with a screenshot.

### Someone in chat is being abusive.
Email safety@overcomersglobalnetwork.com with a screenshot and the channel name. Leaders can remove unsafe messages from moderated channels. For urgent safety concerns, email safety@overcomersglobalnetwork.com.

### How do I give?
Tap the **Give** tab. Giving opens our secure giving page at overcomersglobalnetwork.com/give, processed by Stripe. The app never stores your card details.

### The app uses my location — why?
Only the **Evangelism Maps** feature uses location, and only when you tap to log a visit. You can decline or revoke the location permission in your device's settings; the map will still load.

### I want to turn off notifications.
**Profile → Notifications**, or in your device's system settings under the Overcomers Global Network app.
