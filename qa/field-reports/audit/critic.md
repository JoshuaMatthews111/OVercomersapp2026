## WRONG-S10 — The story badge is not "missing 6pt left and 3pt bottom" — storyRing's borderRadius:43 makes the mask a circle, and the badge's centre falls outside it

**files** app/(tabs)/index.tsx:273, app/(tabs)/index.tsx:283, app/(tabs)/index.tsx:434, app/(tabs)/index.tsx:439, node_modules/expo-linear-gradient/ios/LinearGradientLayer.swift:20, node_modules/expo-linear-gradient/ios/LinearGradientView.swift:10

What S10 missed: `storyRing` (app/(tabs)/index.tsx:434) is `{ width: 86, height: 86, borderRadius: 43, ... }`. Core Animation applies masksToBounds to the layer's bounds WITH its cornerRadius, so the mask is a circle of radius 43 centred at (43, 43) — not the 86x86 square S10 reasoned about.

`storyBadge` (app/(tabs)/index.tsx:439) is `{ position: 'absolute', left: -6, bottom: -3, width: 30, height: 30, borderRadius: 15, borderWidth: 2, ... }`. In the ring's coordinate space that puts the badge at x -6..24 and y 59..89, centre (9, 74). Distance from the mask centre: hypot(43-9, 74-43) = hypot(34, 31) = 46.01. The mask radius is 43. The badge's CENTRE sits 3.0pt outside the circular mask. Only the lune between distance 31.01 and 43 survives — roughly the 12pt of the badge nearest the ring centre, out of 30pt. About 60% of the badge, the whole down-left outer side, is gone.

This is why S10's implied fixes will not work. You cannot set `overflow: 'visible'` on the gradient (masksToBounds is hard-coded native, not driven by the style prop), and nudging the badge back inside the 86x86 square does not help either — at left:0, bottom:0 the centre is (15, 71), distance hypot(28,28) = 39.6, inside the mask but the badge would then be 15pt inside the gold ring and no longer read as a badge on the rim at all.
